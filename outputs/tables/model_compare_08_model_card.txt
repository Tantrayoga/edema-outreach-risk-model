
MODEL COMPARISON SUMMARY

Purpose:
Compare multiple models for ranking edema patients by risk of future high-risk trajectory.

Target:
Future high-risk edema trajectory.

Cohort:
All edema patients in the landmark table.

Age handling:
Age and birth-year features are excluded from model training.
Age group is only used for reporting.

Models tested:
logistic_regression, random_forest, extra_trees, gradient_boosting, hist_gradient_boosting

Selection criterion:
Best model is selected by top 10% precision, with Average Precision as tie-breaker.

Best model:
gradient_boosting

Baseline high-risk rate in test set:
6.99%

Best model top 10% precision:
21.50%

Interpretation:
If top 10% precision is much higher than baseline, the model improves outreach prioritization.
For example, if baseline is 7% and top 10% precision is 22%, the model-selected top 10% group is about 3.1x enriched over random selection.

Recommended framing:
This is a risk-ranking model, not a hard classifier.
The operational question is:
If SVH can contact only the highest-risk 5% or 10% of edema patients, which model creates the most enriched outreach list?

Limitations:
- Not a clinical diagnosis tool.
- VitalStatus does not provide exact death timing.
- External validation is needed.
- False positives may be acceptable if the intervention is low-risk outreach or follow-up.
