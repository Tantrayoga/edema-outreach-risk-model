
MODEL PURPOSE
Rank edema patients by risk of future high-risk trajectory.
This is an outreach prioritization model, not a hard death-prediction model.

COHORT
All edema patients, including patients aged 90+.

AGE HANDLING
Age was NOT used as a model feature.
PatientBirthYearBin, PatientBirthYearBin_num, and age_group were excluded from training.
Age group is reported only descriptively.

TARGET
Future high-risk edema trajectory:
patient eventually marked deceased AND later had long/complex edema-related or utilization trajectory.

PREDICTION POINT
60 days after first edema diagnosis.

MAIN EVALUATION
Top-k precision, recall, and lift over baseline.

BASELINE
Baseline high-risk rate in the test set: 6.99%.

INTERPRETATION
If top 10% precision is substantially higher than baseline, the model can help prioritize outreach.
For example, if baseline is 7% and top 10% precision is 22%, then the top 10% group is about 3.1x enriched over random selection.

LIMITATIONS
- Not a clinical diagnosis tool.
- VitalStatus does not provide exact death timing.
- Age is excluded from the model, but older patients may still have different encounter patterns.
- External validation is needed before deployment.

RECOMMENDED USE
Use predicted_risk to create outreach tiers:
- Top 5%: urgent outreach
- Top 10%: high-priority outreach
- Top 20%: monitor / secondary outreach
