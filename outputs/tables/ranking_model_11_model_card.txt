
MODEL PURPOSE
This model ranks edema patients by risk of future high-risk trajectory.
It is not a hard death-prediction model.

TARGET
Future high-risk edema trajectory:
patient eventually marked deceased AND later had long/complex edema-related or utilization trajectory.

PREDICTION POINT
60 days after first edema diagnosis.

COHORT
All edema patients, including 90+.
Age is represented as broad age_group, not exact birth-year bin.

MAIN EVALUATION
Top-k precision, recall, and lift.

WHY TOP-K?
SVH likely has limited outreach capacity.
The practical question is:
If SVH can only contact the top 5% or 10% highest-risk edema patients, how enriched is that group for future high-risk trajectories?

BASELINE
Baseline high-risk rate in the test set: 6.99%.

KEY RESULTS
See ranking_model_05_topk_precision_recall_lift.csv.

INTERPRETATION TEMPLATE
If the top 10% precision is much higher than the baseline rate, the model is useful for prioritizing outreach.
For example, if baseline is 7% and top 10% precision is 22%, then the top 10% group is about 3.1x enriched compared with random selection.

LIMITATIONS
- This is a prioritization model, not a clinical diagnosis tool.
- VitalStatus does not provide exact death timing.
- External validation is needed before real deployment.
- False positives may be acceptable if the intervention is low-risk outreach, follow-up scheduling, or care coordination.

RECOMMENDED USE
Use predicted_risk to create outreach tiers:
- Top 5%: urgent outreach
- Top 10%: high-priority outreach
- Top 20%: monitor / secondary outreach
